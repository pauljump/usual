Decision date: 2026-09-27. Paul explicitly made a great revenue source with low personal
involvement the objective. This is the operating direction, not a revenue forecast or
evidence that a buyer exists. [SPITE.md](SPITE.md) remains the public voice and experience.

## What counts as success

Optimize for recurring contribution after infrastructure, payment fees, refunds and
delivery/support costs, while tracking Paul's time separately. Revenue alone can disguise
a service job. Traffic, held connections and X reach are acquisition/research measures,
not revenue. Do not convert connection time into invented dollars saved or lost.

No personal income target has been specified. The first commercial milestone is a real
customer paying for a defined repeatable deliverable and then renewing. A provisional
operating target after setup is at most one hour of Paul's involvement per week; this is
a design target, not a user-specified requirement or an achieved result. Track initial
setup/sales effort separately rather than hiding it in an automation claim.

Every proposed feature must name its buyer or acquisition purpose, an observable result,
how it could improve repeat revenue or cost, and what result would make us stop it.

## Working commercial hypothesis

Use public Bait to attract consenting installations and collect distinctive interactions.
Package a narrow, useful result from those interactions for a repeat buyer. The first
candidate is a versioned behavior pack for a hosting or abuse-tool developer: a redacted
observed sequence, its interpretation and uncertainty, a sanitized fixture, and a check
showing how to recognize that behavior in the buyer's own system.

That buyer would pay only if the pack adds useful coverage or saves integration work.
We have not shown either yet. Generic `.env` probes, duplicate password checks and a
simple IP list are unlikely to pass the test. Low-volume raw data is not automatically
valuable. An alternative test-fixture product would require separate developer evidence;
it must not be declared validated by sales in the adjacent intelligence market.

The concrete proposed purchase, sample acceptance gates and owner-time arithmetic
are in [BUYER-TEST.md](BUYER-TEST.md). Its private $250 evaluation hypothesis is not
an approved public price or evidence of willingness to pay. The current synthetic
report is a technical fixture and is not yet eligible for that commercial test.

Design for a standard format and recurring delivery. Custom dashboards, bespoke incident
investigations, daily manual reporting and account-specific integrations fail the desired
operating model unless a reusable capability justifies the initial work. Even standard
data licensing can require substantial sales, procurement and support; measure these.

## Next bounded experiment

The saved public snapshot generated 2026-09-27T13:04:57Z showed 69 distinct credentials
reused and zero recorded linked-maze follow-ups. These are not 69 identified operators.
The latest 20 visible reuse events targeted malformed paths ending `:2083/login/`.
A scripted hosting-login workflow is a hypothesis, not a confirmed toolkit attribution.

1. Verify response compatibility for the observed login workflow. Use bounded inert
   service emulation on owned HTTP endpoints. A generic HTML company may not satisfy
   a scripted client's expectations. Local protocol fixtures prove plumbing only.
2. Compare a promptly completed compatible response with the existing behavior in an
   explicitly identified experiment cohort. Persist assignment and response version;
   analyze by observed sessions/cohorts rather than treating retries as independent people.
   Keep public stable maze URLs deterministic. Do not silently replace all tarpits.
3. Record whether a subsequent request attempts file upload, account creation, mail use,
   export or a command. Accept bounded inert input only: no execution, outbound mail,
   proxies, external callbacks or paid model calls. Minimize and redact collected data.
4. Produce one evidence pack with observation time, exposure provenance, response version,
   sequence, confidence, sanitized fixture and a useful detection/test assertion. Keep
   synthetic validation separate from unsolicited traffic and never invent missing steps.
5. Draft a standard buyer offer around that exact sample. Commercial validation requires
   authorized buyer contact and an actual purchase, followed by renewal; page views or
   compliments do not qualify. No outreach or publication is authorized by this brief.

Proposed research cutoff: inspect the first 100 qualifying login sessions or seven days
after an authorized launch, whichever comes first. A small or duplicated sample may be
inconclusive. If nothing beyond credential checking appears, revisit response fidelity
and traffic coverage before extending the maze. This cutoff is not a scheduled job.
If a sample contains no independently useful information, do not build billing or a
production data API around it.

Local implementation: [HOSTING-EXPERIMENT.md](HOSTING-EXPERIMENT.md). The opt-in
experiment and read-only behavior report exist; activation bindings remain absent.
Synthetic client checks validate the plumbing only. No unsolicited follow-up,
useful paid sample or customer demand has been established by that implementation.

Paul's 2026-09-27 follow-up favors broader investigation before narrowing the product.
[INVESTIGATION.md](INVESTIGATION.md) maps the bait families, attribution evidence,
known blind spots and staged research. A private local report now distinguishes

This expands evidence collection; it does not validate a buyer, identify an operator,

## Owner experience and limits

The intended owner view is a concise summary of cash collected, renewals/churn, operating
cost, delivery failures, support time and the next consequential decision. Unknown cost
must remain unknown; a zero entry needs evidence. Automate deterministic packaging,
deduplication and delivery after demand is demonstrated. Keep manual exceptions visible.

No new spend, paid quota, external publication or deployment was authorized by adopting
the revenue objective. Existing no-spend and deployment-review constraints remain in force.
This research used saved Bait data and public sources; it made no production telemetry
refresh, paid provider call or live experiment. No profit or passive-income guarantee is
possible, and the active objective remains unachieved until there is real business evidence.
