How this experiment works

The connected tools save and retrieve directly from this server. They see only what your assistant passes from the accessible conversation. The manual fallback is a pasted snapshot; it does not auto-sync. This is one private pilot collection, not separate accounts for multiple people.

Usual skips exact duplicate entries. It does not automatically merge paraphrases or resolve conflicting preferences. “Inferred” means the assistant generalized from your words; check those entries carefully.

To correct an entry, use “Edit a copy,” change it, import and accept the replacement, then exclude the old entry. Exclusion stops recall but preserves the record in your export. Imports always require review, including restored exports.

Stored in a separate SQLite database on the host running Usual. No analytics or model API calls. Keep your private link to yourself; it grants access to this collection. Export before moving hosts. Your assistant processes the text you paste under its own account policies. Secret filtering is best-effort; review anything you transfer.
