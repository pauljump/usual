What this demo does

The original chat analyzes your decisions. Usual groups matching rules from those analyses into one profile, preserving the evidence and differences. It doesn’t yet merge differently worded ideas or resolve contradictions automatically.

Patterns are interpretations, not a guarantee of your next choice. To test Usual, ask a fresh chat to predict a decision you’ve held back, then compare it with what you actually chose.

The read-only link works without signing in. Anyone you give it to can read the profile. An assistant’s ability to open private links varies; if it cannot read yours, download the profile and attach it.
